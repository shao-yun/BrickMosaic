BrickMosaic: Multi-Layer LEGO Stylization via Tiling Optimization and Palette-Constrained Rendering

Shao-Yun Lo, Hsiang-Yu Wang, Wei Huang, and Ming-Te Chi

National Chengchi University, Taiwan

Accepted at Computer Graphics International (CGI) 2026 · Presented in London · To appear in Springer LNCS

📊 View Slides 💻 Code (Coming Soon)

Abstract

Transforming a single image into a LEGO®-style mosaic is challenging because the result must preserve semantic structure and visual coherence while respecting discrete brick geometries and a limited color palette.

We present BrickMosaic, a unified multi-layer framework for assembly-aware LEGO stylization. Our method decomposes the input into depth-ordered semantic layers, generates LEGO-aware layouts through tiling optimization with brick decomposition and inner-contour pruning, and applies palette-constrained stylization with a perceptual loss to preserve local structure under the standard 39-color LEGO palette.

Experiments show that BrickMosaic reduces tiling time by over 10× compared with the graph-based baseline TilinGNN while maintaining competitive contour similarity. Compared with general pixelization methods such as MYOS and SD-piXL, our method achieves the best LPIPS score, producing LEGO-style mosaics that are visually faithful and structurally plausible for assembly-aware design.

Pipeline

Our framework transforms an input image into an assembly-aware LEGO model through layered scene representation, LEGO-aware tiling optimization, and perceptual-enhanced palette-constrained LEGO stylization.
